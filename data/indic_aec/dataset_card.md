# Indic Agricultural Extension Corpus

Phase 4 prepares native Hindi, Marathi, Bengali and Tamil agricultural extension text for Phase 5 masked-language modeling. See the [Phase 4 workflow](../../phase_04_indic_aec_corpus/README.md) for acquisition, quality review, preprocessing and evaluation exclusion.

**Release status:** The acquisition and validation pipeline is available. The four-language corpus and corpus-wide statistics are pending collection and documented review. Pilot source and overlap counts do not represent a training release.

Each document retains its source URL, publisher, reuse terms, family and content hashes. Splits keep publication families together and reserve Phase 6 evaluation sources. Source licenses apply individually. ICAR Kheti pilot sources carry noncommercial and share-alike terms. Review distribution rights for each source before publishing extracted text.

The overlap audit checks normalized text against verified Phase 6 QA passages and reconstructed full pages. It cannot establish that translated or heavily paraphrased copies are absent. OCR output requires review against the original pages.
